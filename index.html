Stanford University School of Medicine

Visiting Researcher · Jul 2026-Present

Researching uncertainty quantification for 4D Flow MRI and 4D-FlowP pressure difference estimation with Prof. Daniel B. Ennis.

  • Modeling measured acceleration as true signal plus deterministic bias and random error, separating systematic effects from scan-to-scan uncertainty.
  • Studying how spatial covariance, including off-diagonal voxel correlations, propagates through a pressure solver into uncertainty for estimated pressure differences.
  • Building a validation roadmap across numerical baselines, in silico 4D-FlowP experiments with CFD truth, and in vitro U-bend repeat scans.

Militech Challenge

One-handed drone-control HMI · Signal processing · Flight interface

A control-oriented wearable HMI project motivated by MUM-T operations: replacing a two-handed controller with a single-hand smart glove so the other hand remains free for communication, cover, or mission tasks.

  • Built around CNT strain sensors that translate finger and wrist motion into electrical signals for command inference.
  • Designed a signal-processing pipeline for noise filtering, baseline drift tracking, finger-coupling compensation, intent recovery, and confidence-gated command generation.
  • Mapped restored hand intent into flight-control commands through a joystick-input layer with arming, tactical modes, emergency stop, and receiver-side safety logic.

Simulation results in the draft report showed reduced finger cross-coupling, stable drift handling, blocked antagonistic inputs, and emergency-stop behavior; human-factor and full flight evaluation remain proposed scope.

SNUQuant

WorldQuant BRAIN collaboration · Alpha research automation

Participated in SNUQuant's 2025-2 WorldQuant BRAIN collaboration, where the team built research infrastructure for automated alpha simulation, screening, and analysis.

  • Worked around the SNUQuant Alpha Machine concept: session management, local alpha databases, batch simulation, expression sanity checks, and submission screening.
  • Explored algorithmic alpha discovery through genetic algorithms, Pareto and NSGA-II style selection, surrogate models, and diversity-aware search.
  • Studied LLM-assisted research workflows, template generation, and robustness metrics for deciding which simulated alphas remain reliable out of sample.
